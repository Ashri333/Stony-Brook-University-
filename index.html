About Stevens

Innovation on the Hudson

Founded in 1870, Stevens Institute of Technology is a premier private research university situated directly across from the Manhattan skyline.

Stevens blends rigorous engineering, computer science, business, and humanities degrees with unmatched access to New York City industry partners and tech hubs.

NYC Advantage

Intern in Manhattan while living in safe, walkable Hoboken.
